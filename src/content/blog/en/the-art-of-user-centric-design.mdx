## Empathy is Key

The first step to user-centric design is understanding your users. This means going beyond demographics and getting into their mindset. What are their goals? What are their pain points? What motivates them?

### Techniques for Building Empathy

*   **User Personas:** Create fictional characters representing your target users.
*   **Empathy Maps:** Visualize what a user says, thinks, does, and feels.
*   **User Interviews:** Talk to real users to get direct feedback.

By putting the user at the center of your design process, you create products that are not only beautiful but also truly useful and enjoyable.
